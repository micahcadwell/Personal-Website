Micah Cadwell is a jazz guitarist and educator with over two decades of experience performing and teaching across the country. His playing draws from the bebop tradition while embracing the harmonic language of post-bop, Brazilian music, and contemporary jazz.

\

Based in Atlanta, Micah has performed at venues and festivals throughout the Southeast and beyond. He brings the same passion to the bandstand as he does to the lesson room \'97 clear, direct, and deeply musical.

\

Working exclusively with intermediate and advanced players, Micah focuses on unlocking new harmonic and improvisational territory \'97 tailoring each lesson to the specific goals and voice of the student.
